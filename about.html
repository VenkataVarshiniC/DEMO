About This Course

BMGT748 is the capstone course for Information Systems (IS) majors, where students apply their knowledge to develop real-world IS solutions for business clients. Through a complete IS development process, students design and implement functional prototypes that solve practical business challenges.

This course prepares students for careers in technology and consulting by bridging the gap between academic theory and industry practice through hands-on project experience.
